Questions from cleaning businesses.

Can customers book Morning or Afternoon?

Yes. You choose how many jobs you take each morning and afternoon, and full time slots are greyed out on your website calendar.

I have mostly regular clients. Will this save me time?

Yes. Pick the client, and their usual details and next date are filled in. Set it to every week or every 2 weeks and add the next visits in one go.

How does the free trial work?

You get the full Crew plan free for 30 days, no credit card. Then pick Solo ($19), Crew ($49) or Crew Plus ($99) a month.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The live demo opens a made-up cleaning business in your browser. Nothing is saved or seen by anyone.
