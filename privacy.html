Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Dayzift is business-management software for small service businesses, provided by LIT America LLC (“we”). This page explains what information Dayzift collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email support@dayzift.com.

Who uses Dayzift

Business owners create a private workspace for their business and invite their crew. Each business decides what it records about its own jobs and customers. For that customer information, the business is responsible for it and we handle it on the business’s behalf.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them).
  • Business details: business name, trade, city, phone, and settings such as alert contacts.
  • Work records: jobs, booking requests, client names, addresses and phone numbers, prices, payment status, job costs such as materials, expenses, receipt photos, and rewards points.
  • Invoices: the client’s name, email, phone and billing address, the invoice lines and totals, and payments the business records. Dayzift does not process payments or store card or bank details.
  • Location during shifts: when a crew member starts a shift, their device’s location is shared with their business until they end the shift. In the phone app this can continue while the app is in the background during a shift. Location is not collected when the person is off shift.
  • Shift log: each shift’s start and end time, a location point about every five minutes during the shift, and where the device was when a job was started or finished. The business’s owners and that crew member can see it. Owners use it to check hours against pay, and can correct a shift’s times; a correction is recorded with the original times.
  • Pay details (owners only): how each crew member is paid, and for employees the Form W-4 choices the owner enters (filing status and amounts) so Dayzift can estimate withholding. Payroll reviews, approvals and recorded payments are kept with who made them and when. Dayzift does not store Social Security numbers or bank details.
  • Device information: a notification token so owners can receive booking alerts on their phone, and basic technical logs needed to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show the job board, crew shifts, books and insights to the right people in each business.
  • To send notices the business has turned on, such as booking-request emails, texts and phone notifications.
  • To email invoices and overdue reminders to a business’s clients when the business asks us to.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and provide support.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Inside a business: owners can see everything in their business, including the shift log. Crew members see only their own jobs, their own shift and their own shift log; their location is visible to the business owners while they’re on shift.
  • A business’s clients: each invoice has a private link the business sends to its client. Anyone with that link can see that one invoice, and the business can see when it was first opened.
  • Service providers: we store data with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and use providers that deliver email, text messages and phone notifications. They process data only to provide those services to us.
  • When required: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or the security of the service.

Camera and photos

The phone app asks for camera and photo access only so you can attach receipt photos to expenses. Photos are stored with that business’s records.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information while the business account is active. A business owner can delete jobs, expenses and team members at any time. To delete your account, or a whole business and its records, email support@dayzift.com from the address on the account and we’ll remove it, except anything we must keep by law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Each business’s records are kept separate, and access is limited to people that business has added. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

Children

Dayzift is a tool for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we’ll update the date above and let business owners know.
