Delete your DayZift account

Last updated October 30, 2026

You can ask us to delete your DayZift account at any time, from the app or from this page. We delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.

How to ask

  1. In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete my account. It brings you to this page with your email filled in.
  2. By email: tap the button below, or write to support@dayzift.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”.

Email a deletion request

We only act on requests sent from the email address on the account, so nobody else can delete it.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile: your name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Crew members: your shift log and location history are deleted, and your name is removed from the team. Jobs and pay records the business must keep for its own taxes stay with the business, without your sign-in.
  • Business owners: if you ask us to delete the whole business, we also delete its jobs, customers, bookings, invoices, expenses, receipts, pay records and team.

What we may keep

  • Billing records for paid plans, kept as long as tax law requires (usually 7 years).
  • Backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.

Questions? Email support@dayzift.com. See also our privacy policy.
