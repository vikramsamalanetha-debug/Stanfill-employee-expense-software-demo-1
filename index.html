What Dazift does

Less juggling between texts, notebooks and spreadsheets.

Everything a small crew business runs on, in one app that works on a laptop in the office and a phone on the job. These are real screens from Dazift, filled with sample data.

Booking requests you won’t miss

Requests from your website land in Dazift and alert every owner by email, text or phone notification, with reminders until someone answers.

A job board for the whole crew

Turn a request into a job, put one or more people on it, and watch it move from new to done. Each crew member sees their own jobs on their phone.

Crew shifts and locations

Crew members start a shift and the office sees where they are, with one tap to route to the next job. Every shift is logged with its hours, route and job check-ins, so you can check hours before you pay. Sharing stops the moment the shift ends.

Books that keep themselves

Finished jobs count as income automatically. Log expenses with a photo of the receipt, track who still owes you, and export the month for your accountant.

Insights at a glance

See earnings against expenses by day, week or month, which services sell, your busiest days, top clients and how each crew member is doing.

Rewards that bring clients back

Customers earn points on every paid job and redeem them for discounts. You see every member’s balance and history.

Projects and subcontractors

Run a remodel or build as one project with phases, a lead on site and crews on each phase. Track costs against the contract, and get warned before a sub’s insurance or license runs out.

Payroll from the board

Hours and jobs from the board turn into pay: hourly with overtime, per job, percent of the job or day rate. W-2 tax estimates, take-home pay, and a 1099 list for January. Dazift never moves your money.

Questions

Good to know.

Do my crew need their own logins?

Yes, each person signs up with their own email. Add them on your Team tab first, and Dazift links them to your business when they register.

Is location tracked all the time?

No. A crew member’s location is shared only while they’re on shift, and ending the shift stops it right away. Each shift’s hours and route are kept in a log the owner and that crew member can see.

Is there a phone app?

Yes. Dazift works on any phone's web browser today, and our iPhone and Android apps are coming to the App Store and Google Play.

Can I have more than one owner?

Yes. Invite a partner or manager as a co-owner and they get full access, including the books.
