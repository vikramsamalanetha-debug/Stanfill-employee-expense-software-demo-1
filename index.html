Invoices

Get paid faster.

Turn finished jobs into an invoice in one tap, with billable materials and markup, sales tax, deposits and retainage. Email or text the link, see when it's opened, and let Dayzift send the overdue reminders. Clients pay you directly.

Job board

Every job. Every crew. One board.

Turn a request into a job, put one or more people on it, and watch it move from new to done. Each crew member sees their own jobs on their phone.

Shifts

Know who’s on the clock.

Crew members start a shift and the office sees where they are, with one tap to route to the next job. Every shift is logged with its hours, route and job check-ins, so you can check hours before you pay. Sharing stops the moment the shift ends.

Payroll

Payroll, worked out for you.

Hours and jobs from the board turn into pay: hourly with overtime, per job, percent of the job or day rate. W-2 tax estimates, take-home pay, and a 1099 list for January. Dayzift never moves your money.

Books

Books that keep themselves.

Finished jobs count as income automatically. Materials, rentals and subs logged on a job count as its costs, so you see each job's profit. Snap receipts and export the month for your accountant.

Projects

Big jobs, under control.

Run a remodel or build as one project with phases, a lead on site and crews on each phase. Track costs against the contract, and get warned before a sub’s insurance or license runs out.

Requests

Never miss a booking.

Requests from your website land in Dayzift and alert every owner by email, text or phone notification, with reminders until someone answers.

Insights

See how you’re really doing.

See earnings against expenses by day, week or month, which services sell, your busiest days, top clients and how each crew member is doing.

Rewards

Bring clients back.

Customers earn points on every paid job and redeem them for discounts. You see every member’s balance and history.

Good to know.

Do my crew need their own logins?

Yes, each person signs up with their own email. Add them on your Team tab first, and Dayzift links them to your business when they register.

Is location tracked all the time?

No. A crew member’s location is shared only while they’re on shift, and ending the shift stops it right away. Each shift’s hours and route are kept in a log the owner and that crew member can see.

Is there a phone app?

Yes. Dayzift works on any phone's web browser today, and our iPhone and Android apps are coming to the App Store and Google Play.

Does Dayzift handle payments?

No. Dayzift writes and sends your invoices and tracks who has paid, but the money goes straight to you: cash, check, Zelle, or your own Stripe, Square or PayPal link.

Can I have more than one owner?

Yes. Invite a partner or manager as a co-owner and they get full access, including the books.
