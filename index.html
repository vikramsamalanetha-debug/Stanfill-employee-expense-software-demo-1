Questions

Good to know.

How does the free trial work?

You get the full Crew plan free for 30 days. No credit card. Before it ends we'll remind you, and you pick Solo, Crew or Crew Plus. If you don't, your account pauses; your data isn't deleted.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Tap “Try the live demo” and pick your trade. It opens a full made-up business in your browser. Nothing you do there is saved or seen by anyone.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are running in an afternoon: add your services, invite your crew by email, and set your booking calendar. Prefer us to do it? Ask about the $499 done-for-you setup.

Can I bring in my customers from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Send us your list and we'll bring it in for you during setup.

How do bookings from my website work?

Your website gets a booking calendar connected to Dayzift: customers pick an open day and time, and the request lands in your app with an alert. We can connect your existing site or build you a new one.

Why do you approve new businesses?

Every business is checked by a real person before it goes live, so your customers and crew know the business on the other end is real. It usually takes less than a day, and your 30 days start once you're approved, so you don't lose any of your trial.

Do my crew need their own logins?

Yes, each person signs up with their own email or phone. Add them on your Team page first, and Dayzift links them to your business when they register.

Is crew location tracked all the time?

No. Location is shared only while someone is on shift, and ending the shift stops it right away.

Do I or my crew need to install anything?

No. Dayzift runs in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Your crew opens the link, signs in and they're ready.

Does Dayzift handle payments or payroll?

No. Clients pay you directly, and your crew are paid the way you already pay them. Dayzift tracks it all and gives you estimates and reports; it doesn't move money or file taxes.

Can I cancel or take my data with me?

Yes, any time. Export your books and invoices as CSV or PDF whenever you like.

Who is behind Dayzift?

Dayzift is a product of LIT America, a software and web company in West Tennessee. Email support@dayzift.com and a real person answers.
